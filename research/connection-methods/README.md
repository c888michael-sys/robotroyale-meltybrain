## Brief findings

Electrical contact, terminal retention, connector mating retention, wire anchoring and component-body mounting are separate load paths **(reasoning)**. The report surveys bolted/printed/interlocking/bonded joints, electrical terminations, locking/strain-relief methods, staking/potting/coating and internal mounts without selecting a design.

The illustrative calculation gives **26.3 N** of steady restraint load for **5 g at 30 mm and 4,000 RPM**, about **537 g** acceleration **(reasoning)**. It does not bound a collision load. Sensor mounting also affects geometry and measured motion; the report includes an idealized radius-shift calculation.

Original logs describe connector/module loss, broken wires/leads, tire retention, bearing/mount failures and cracked fastener/enclosure regions. Failed repairs, proposed changes, modeled results and limited fight successes remain labelled. There is no basis for a statistical frequency ranking or a universal strength rating.

**Status:** Source research and calculations completed. No product/supplier choices, final joint design, hardware tests or combat qualification. The requirement was first recorded on 8 November 2026; this report expands that folder's original research scope.
